What happens after the inquiry, and when.

Five stages, in order. You do the first one. We do the rest and show you every step.

The five stages

1

Intake

You tell us your songs.

WhenAfter you send the inquiry form.

2

Audit

We check each song's registrations at The MLC, your PRO and the societies.

WhenOnce we have your song list.

3

Register

We file whatever is missing.

WhenOnce you've signed and the audit shows a gap.

4

Recover

We claim royalties that may be uncollected, back as far as the rules allow.

WhenAfter each registration is accepted.

5

Report

We send you a report of every song's status.

WhenFrom the first registration on.

Switching from another admin

If you already have an admin, nothing changes until you say so. We look at your current agreement first, including when it ends and what notice it needs, and we only file for songs and territories that are free to move.
