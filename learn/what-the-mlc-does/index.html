What The MLC does, and why it may be holding your mechanicals

The MLC (the Mechanical Licensing Collective) collects the mechanical royalties that US streaming services pay for the songs they stream, and pays them to the songwriters and publishers it can match to each song.

What "unmatched" means

A streaming service reports a recording. The MLC then has to find which song that recording uses and who wrote it. When it can't, because a song was never registered or its details don't line up, the money is held as unmatched.

Why it may be yours

Unmatched money is held for a time so writers and publishers can claim it. If your songs aren't registered with The MLC, or are registered with missing writers or shares, some of your mechanicals may be among it.

What to do about it

  • Check that each song is registered at The MLC with every writer and share.
  • Check that your recordings are matched to those songs.
  • Claim what was held, as far back as the rules allow.
