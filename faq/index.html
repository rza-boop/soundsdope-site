Getting started

What does soundsdope.io do?

We register your songs at The MLC, your PRO and the societies, then claim royalties that may be uncollected on them.

How do I start?

Send the inquiry form. We reply by email.

Your songs and royalties

What does "may be uncollected" mean?

Royalties your songs earned that haven't reached you, often because a song isn't registered or its details don't match.

How far back can you claim?

As far as each society's rules allow. It differs from one society to the next.

Is the calculator's range what I'll be paid?

No. It is an estimate before we've looked at your catalog, not a quote or a promise of payment.
